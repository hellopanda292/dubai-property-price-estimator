What drives the price permutation importance

  1. 1

    Clean real data

    Official Dubai Land Department transactions. Removed gifts, lease-to-own and bulk developer registrations, impossible sizes and extreme prices.

  2. 2

    Gradient boosting

    400 small decision trees (scikit-learn) predict the price per sq ft from area, type, bedrooms, size and off-plan status. Areas are encoded by their typical price.

  3. 3

    Runs in your browser

    The trees are exported to JSON and evaluated by a few lines of JavaScript, so estimates are instant and nothing is sent to a server.
